# IGT Learning Context

## Language

**My Learning**: The learning-history and reflection area, comprising Overview, Handbook, and Coach across Web and CLI.

**Overview**: The overall picture of all recorded learning activity, with distinct measures for each kind of activity and a recent-activity timeline.

**Saved Grammar check**: A Grammar check whose original text and feedback the learner explicitly saved. Unsaved checks, sentence-translation Practice attempts, and full-text analysis are separate activities.

**Handbook**: The chronological history of saved Grammar checks, searchable and filterable by mistake type. One record represents one saved check, including checks with no diagnosed mistake.
_Avoid_: Generated error report, grammar-rule library

**No diagnosed mistakes**: A saved Grammar check with no recorded diagnoses. This describes the saved feedback, not a guarantee of error-free writing or durable mastery.

**Coach**: Analysis of recurring mistakes in saved Grammar checks, with possible underlying causes, correct grammar principles, and supporting examples available on demand.
_Avoid_: Learning-plan manager, task scheduler

**Mistake pattern**: A recurring grammar issue observed in saved Grammar checks. Its frequency describes those records and does not establish overall proficiency or durable mastery.

**Possible root cause**: An evidence-linked hypothesis explaining a mistake pattern, rather than a confirmed psychological cause.
